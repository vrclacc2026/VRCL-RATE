③ FORMULA SYSTEM

Formula me ye variables use kar sakte ho:

OLD = us packing ka old rate
CHANGE = us packing ka manually defined change
OLD15 = 15 KG old rate
CHANGE15KG = 15 KG me diya gaya change
NEW15 = 15 KG new rate
OLD1LTR = 1 LTR old rate
CHANGE1LTR = 1 LTR me diya gaya change
NEW1LTR = 1 LTR new rate

Examples:
15 KG se ₹15 change aur formula:
CHANGE15KG * 0.5

1 LTR ke change ka half:
CHANGE1LTR * 0.5

1 LTR ke change ka 25%:
CHANGE1LTR * 0.25

Old rate + 1 LTR change:
OLD + CHANGE1LTR
